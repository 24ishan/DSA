<h5>Q :  What is this project about ?</h5><br>
A : I built a smart monitoring system that helps nurses keep track of patients in real time and respond faster to urgent health needs. When a patient's vital signs reach a dangerous level, the system immediately flags the issue and pulls up their complete medical history. It then processes this information to give nurses instant, plain-English summary notes and care recommendations. Nurses can also chat directly with the system to ask questions about a patient's condition or treatment options. This gives healthcare staff immediate, reliable guidance so they can make safer decisions when every second counts.
